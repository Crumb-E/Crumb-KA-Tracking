### Motivation
I've been trying to get a Software Engineer job (and any other kind) for a while. Clearly something isn't working. I decided to reflect on why this is the case and have come to the following conclusions. While I don't believe the hype around AI completely replacing programmers. AI combined with the hiring bubble popping, and a generally middling global economy, has caused a contraction in the market. A market that was already oversaturated at that.

A note on AI. I have come to essentially view it as adjacent to the venerable art of copy-pasting from the internet until you get the result you want. Something most companies seemed okay with. AI is similar. It scrapes the internet for data and puts together output that is acceptable. Except it's automated now. It shares a lot of the same drawbacks with scalability, security, and difficulty getting specific results. But it worked for companies before, makes sense AI works for them now. At least when used in that role. The companies demanding as much token usage as possible are still ridiculous. Seriously it's literally telling employees "please spend as much of my money as feasibly possible."

I was unhappy with this approach to relentlessly copying existing solutions anyway. So I had been drawn to low level design which reignited a passion for programming I didn't even know had dimmed.

It is a more specialized field. In the realm of a masters degree. So less competitive in the saturated employment field. AI isn't as good a fit in my view, and seemingly others. Where bad code is referred to as "blowing your foot off" and the drawbacks on scalability, security, and specificity seem most impactful. Anecdotally, many llms struggle with it to the point of shutting down and refusing to work on C++ codebases.

Oh, also I need a portfolio to show I am as skilled as I say I am.

To this end I have set out with the goals of learning low level development and building a portfolio with it. I am passionate about my field and do not want to throw my hands up without giving it a proper go. Also regardless I intend to use these skills on personal projects. So I have resolved to construct a portfolio.
